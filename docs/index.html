Welcome to RUGIPO

Rufus Giwa Polytechnic, Owo is a leading tertiary institution in Ondo State, training technicians and technologists across Applied Science, Engineering, and Management Studies since 1979. This portal brings admissions, fee payment, and student records into one secure place.

🌍

Our Vision

  • Global Recognition — to be a leading technological institution recognized internationally for its quality, relevance, and impact.
  • Local & Global Impact — driving effective training and research that develops people and creates meaningful differences locally in Ondo State, across Nigeria, and worldwide.
🎯

Our Mission

  • Stimulate Excellence — foster a strong spirit of excellence in teaching, research, scholarship, and creative work among both staff and students.
  • Uphold Values — encourage academic freedom, innovative thought, high ethical standards, integrity, accountability, and social justice.
  • Manpower Development — train skilled middle-level manpower in applied sciences, engineering, management, and technology-related fields to support national growth.
⭐

Core Values

  • Integrity — Commitment to integrity and high ethical standards.
  • Excellence — Spirit of excellence in all academic and creative pursuits.
  • Innovation — Promotion of hard work, teamwork, originality, and self-reliance.
